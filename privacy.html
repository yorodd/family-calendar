Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Family Calendar is a personal app used by one household on a single home computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app uses

  • Google Calendar events (titles, times, locations) from the calendars the account owner chooses, so they can be shown on the home screen.
  • Events created on the home screen, which the app saves to the chosen Google calendar.
  • A Google sign-in token, so the app can stay connected without signing in each time.

Where it is stored

All data, including the sign-in token, is stored only on the household's own computer. The app has no server, database or website that receives your data.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app has no analytics or tracking.

Google API Services

Family Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the app at any time from its Settings screen, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the computer removes everything it has stored.
