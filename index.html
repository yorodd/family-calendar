Family Calendar

A private family calendar that runs on one computer in our home. It shows our family's Google Calendar events on a touchscreen, and keeps our chores, meal plans and shopping lists in one place.

Who it's for: our own household only. It is not offered to the public.

What it does with Google: it reads and adds events in the Google calendars we choose, so they appear on the home screen and on our phones.

Privacy: see the privacy policy.
